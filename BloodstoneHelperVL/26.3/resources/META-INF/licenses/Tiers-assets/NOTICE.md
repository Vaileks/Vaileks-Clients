# Tier icon provenance
The MCTiers and PvPTiers gamemode PNGs originate from the official PvPTiers/Tiers resource pack (master, retrieved 2026-10-19), licensed GPL-3.0. Original PNG source files are supplied in source/, alongside COPYING. BloodHelper scales these standalone artwork files to 32x32 for its bitmap font. No Java implementation was copied from Tiers.
CIS Tiers icons: https://cistiers.com/api/config and https://cdn.cistiers.com/tierlists/.
ATiers icons: https://cdn.atiers.net/atiers_assets/icons/new/.
MyTiers icons: https://mytiers.ru/api/guilds and https://mytiers.ru/tier_icons/.
Names and artwork identify the respective independent rating services; BloodHelper does not claim their authorship or official endorsement.
